01 / THE CHALLENGE

FOOD IS PREPARED.
FOOD IS WASTED.

And too often, nobody sees the connection until it is too late. The issue is bigger than leftovers — it is a disconnected decision-making process.

01 — INVISIBLE DEMAND
ATTENDANCE VARIES

Demand keeps changing.

Attendance, preferences, bookings and campus events make daily meal requirements difficult to estimate.

02 — OVERPRODUCTION
PREPARED ≠ CONSUMED

Preparation misses the mark.

Uncertain demand can lead to excess preparation, wasted ingredients and avoidable costs.

03 — DISCONNECTED SYSTEMS
Kitchen×Tracking×Rescue

Useful data stays apart.

Kitchen operations, waste tracking and surplus redistribution often work independently.

THE REAL GAPNo connected intelligence linking demand, preparation, consumption and recovery.↗
